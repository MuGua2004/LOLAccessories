**English**

**New Items (5 Legendaries)**
- **Ravenous Hydra**: +65 AD, +15 ability haste, +12% lifesteal. Cleave (40% AD splash) and Ravenous Crescent active (80% AD AoE), both with slash FX
- **Thornmail**: +150 HP, +75 armor. Thorns reflect 20 + 10% bonus armor nature magic damage and apply 40% Grievous Wounds
- **Trinity Force**: +36 AD, +30% attack speed and equal draw speed, +333 HP, +15% CDR. Spellblade and Quicken
- **Warmog's Armor**: +1000 HP, +100% base health regen. Warmog's Vigor (bonus health = 12% of equipped health) and Warmog's Heart (at 2000 bonus health, restore 3% max health per second after 8s out of combat)
- **Heartsteel**: +900 HP. Goliath (3% physical damage reduction per 1000 max health) and Colossal Consumption (a three-ring sigil mark matures over 3s; shatter it to deal 70 + 6% max health physical damage and permanently gain 10% of it as max health, with its signature shatter sound)

**Improvements**
- Multiple Spellblade items now stack damage and share a combined cooldown (pattern reused for Energized items later)
- Thorn reflection now deals nature-school magic damage
- Tooltips streamlined: mechanic explanations removed, numbers and cooldowns kept
- Test dummies added (/summon lolaccessories:test_brute and test_player_dummy, for testing only)
